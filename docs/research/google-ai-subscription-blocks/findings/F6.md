## Conflicting evidence

- Gemini CLI FAQ still describes Pro/Ultra higher limits in Gemini CLI, while the 2026-05-19 blog and the FAQ's own banner say consumer serving ended 2026-06-18. Dates reconcile it: the earlier subscription FAQ body is stale; the cutoff and banner supersede it. Current state = Gemini CLI no longer serves Pro/Ultra; Antigravity CLI is the consumer path.
- CLIProxyAPI docs pages are present in web-search index but 404 live (2026-11-18). Mechanics reported here were taken from first-party source files instead of those docs; no claim rests on the stale pages.
- Vendor framing vs practitioner framing: Google FAQ calls third-party OAuth piggybacking a violation; HN/relay authors describe the same pattern as a legitimate way to use an already-paid subscription. This is a framing conflict, not a factual one — both describe the same technical flow.
- Within relay READMEs, gating signals differ for overlapping Google models: dvcrn returns 410 for Claude 4.6 / GPT-OSS-120B; vlelyavin says those were removed upstream on 2026-11-18 and that Gemini 4 Argon + Claude 5.5 need paid non-trial Pro/Ultra; cpa-plugin's catalog still lists `gemini-3.5-flash` and several 3.x previews. Which exclusions are upstream account entitlements vs per-relay policy choices is unresolved.
- sub2api #1515 commenters disagree on the 403 cause: one reports phone verification (`VALIDATION_REQUIRED`) as the fix; others report re-authorization still fails and that the 403 invalidates the authorization. No vendor confirmation either way.
- Nautili's 2026-11-06 test reports Google refusing the individual account through the gateway, while multiple relay READMEs claim working Pro/Ultra subscriptions. Plausibly account/license state and post-cutoff timing, but not established.
