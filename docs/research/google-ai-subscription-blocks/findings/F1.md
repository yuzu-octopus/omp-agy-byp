## Angle

F1 — Google consumer-subscription entitlement & official-client boundary. Question: what Google *officially* documents about where Gemini app / Google AI Pro / Ultra entitlements apply, and how it separates consumer-subscription access from Gemini API / Google AI Studio / Vertex AI paid API access. All dates below are doc-shown dates; all pages fetched 2026-11-18 unless noted.

Bottom line (official text only): Google does **not** publish a standalone "Gemini Apps Terms of Service" today (2026-11-18). Gemini Apps are governed by the **Google Terms of Service** + **Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy** (+ feature-specific addenda such as Agentic Calling). The consumer/API split is documented in three places: (1) the Privacy Hub's closed enumeration of what "Gemini Apps" includes, (2) the Gemini API Additional ToS line "not for consumer use", and (3) the AI Studio "Google AI plans" doc's "AI Studio UI only" limitation.

## Conflicting evidence

- **Subscription "includes AI Studio" vs "API billed separately":** Google One plan pages advertise "Expanded access to Gemini 4 Argon in Google Antigravity and Google AI Studio" and "AI Studio with expanded limits to prototype, experiment, and build ...", and the AI Pro benefits page lists "Google AI Studio". Simultaneously the AI Studio "Google AI plans" doc says benefits "apply only within the Google AI Studio web interface" and "Google bills and manages Gemini API usage (such as using API keys or external applications) separately" (C12). Reconciliation: subscription unlocks the AI Studio **UI/Playground/Build**, not API-key access or production API quota. Both are primary; not a contradiction.
- **"not for consumer use" (API ToS) vs consumer subscription marketed for developers:** C9 vs C23 plan benefits (Colab, Android Studio, Antigravity). Reconciliation: the ToS phrase defines the API/AI Studio *service* purpose; AI Pro/Ultra are consumer Google One subscriptions that include developer-facing Google products, while Gemini API paid tier remains separately billed (C10, C12, C21).
- **Community "automation violates ToS" vs literal ToS text:** C24 states a blanket rule; C4 conditions the automated-access prohibition on violating machine-readable instructions, and C8 targets generated content. Treat C24 as practitioner interpretation, not official policy.
- **Standalone "Gemini Apps Terms of Service":** task target named it; live official sources (C1, C2) show the current structure uses Google ToS + Gen AI PUP instead. No primary source found that publishes such a standalone document on 2026-11-18.
